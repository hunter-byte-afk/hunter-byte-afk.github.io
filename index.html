Who am I?

Hello everyone! My name is Renae Hunt and I am an undergraduate at New Mexico State University studying for my B.A. in Computer Science and my minor in art. I am an aspiring game designer that has made small video games in class but one day hope to do something bigger than that. I love experimenting with art and how we can combine that with technology.

Projects

Virtute Will Have You.

This is currently a WIP project that will showcase 3 small steel sculptures made out of scrap found in the NMSU art studio. This is losely inspired by "Plea from a Cat Named Virtute" and "Virtute the Cat Explains Her Departure" by The Weakerthans. It is meant to showcase longing for something to change and dealing with grief from losing a pet.

untitled.
